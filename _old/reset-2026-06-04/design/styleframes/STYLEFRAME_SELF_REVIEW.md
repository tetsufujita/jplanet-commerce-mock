## Three-Axis Review

### 1. Investor Narrative Clarity — 8/10

The six frames now show a clear sequence: regional ambition, commerce friction, real operating wedge, two-layer moat, expansion rails, and protocol close. The investor can read "not a store, not a chatbot, but infrastructure" from the sequence.

Remaining risk: Frame C still needs more "real evidence" texture in a later pass, ideally with verified operational artifact styling rather than generic route nodes.

### 2. Static Visual Quality — 7/10

Frame A, D, E, and F have a strong enough premium direction. Frame B is structurally useful but still a little too much like a clean consulting diagram. Frame C is clear, but the operating proof can become more distinctive.

Revision already done: Frame E was changed from card-like roadmap blocks to a darker rail expansion visual.

### 3. Motion Readiness — 8/10

Each frame maps cleanly to a motion idea:

- A: line draw and type reveal
- B: fragments drifting and failing to align
- C: one order path resolving through operations
- D: Layer 1 separating from Layer 2
- E: rails branching across phases
- F: graph simplifying into final Andes close

The frames are suitable for Remotion blocking.

## Hallmark Six-Axis Review

- Philosophy: 5/5
  - The frames express Andes as market-built infrastructure, not a reference-site clone.

- Hierarchy: 4/5
  - The narrative sequence is clear. Headline and visual system do not compete too heavily.

- Execution: 4/5
  - HTML/SVG source is reusable for later Remotion and Web primitives. Frame C needs richer evidence treatment later.

- Specificity: 5/5
  - Uses Andes-specific and Brazil/LATAM-specific anchors: Japan/Brazil corridor, J-Planet, CNPJ, PRC, SISCOMEX, NF-e, PIX, customs, logistics, entity chain.

- Restraint: 4/5
  - Crimson remains pin-point. Motion implications are meaningful rather than decorative.

- Variety: 4/5
  - Frames include corridor, friction field, operating route, layer stack, expansion rails, and protocol graph. Frame B/C need future refinement to avoid feeling too similar as abstract systems diagrams.

## Verdict

Pass for concept/styleframe v1.

Not final art direction yet. The next step should be a stronger visual pass on Frame B and Frame C, then a Remotion motion board for the full sequence.
